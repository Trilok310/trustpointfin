Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Demat account?
A Demat (Dematerialized) account is a digital account used to hold shares and securities electronically. It is mandatory for investing in the Indian stock market.
What documents are required to open an account?
You will typically need your PAN card, Aadhaar card (linked to a mobile number), a canceled cheque or bank statement, and a digital signature.
How long does account opening take?
The digital KYC process takes only a few minutes. Once submitted, your account is usually activated within 24 to 48 hours after verification by the exchange.
What is an Authorized Partner?
An Authorized Partner acts as an official representative of the broker (Angel One). We help facilitate your onboarding and provide dedicated support, while your funds and securities are securely held with Angel One and CDSL/NSDL.
