Frequently Asked Questions

Is it mandatory to have a Demat account?

Yes. A Demat (Dematerialized) account is absolutely mandatory for holding shares and securities in electronic format in India. You cannot buy or sell stocks without one.

Why is 2026 a good time to open a Demat account?

2026 has seen unprecedented structural growth in the Indian markets, driven by domestic institutional inflows and a rising retail participation rate. Opening a Demat account now allows investors to capture long-term compounding benefits.
