## 1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES

TrustPoint Finance Academy provides objective, rigorous, and regulator-aligned financial literacy. The curriculum teaches how modern capital markets function, balancing return opportunities with structural risks.

This policy defines the vocabulary standards across all digital and print content surfaces. It is designed to be **context-aware and semantically precise**, ensuring that:
1. Misleading financial promises, affirmative return guarantees, and get-rich-quick fallacies are strictly eliminated.
2. Legitimate financial terminology (such as *Risk-Free Rate*, *Cost of Capital*, *Speculation*, and *Volatility*) is preserved and protected.
3. Educational refutations and myth-busting negations (e.g., *"No strategy can guarantee profits"*) are recognized as beneficial and fully approved.
4. The colloquial gambling vocabulary of the *Satta* family is eliminated with zero tolerance, and never reintroduced via poorly formulated replacements.
5. Technical analysis tools (Moving Averages, RSI, VWAP) are described exclusively as descriptive/analytical lagging indicators, never predictive guarantees.

## 3. RESTRICTED VOCABULARY SPECIFICATIONS

### Category 1: Affirmative Guarantee Claims (Level 3 — Hard Block)
Affirmative assertions that an investment, trading setup, or security eliminates risk or delivers guaranteed financial returns.

**Restricted English Phrases:**
- `guaranteed return` / `guaranteed returns`
- `guaranteed profit` / `guaranteed income`
- `guaranteed wealth` / `guaranteed capital`
- `assured return` / `assured profit`
- `sure profit` / `sure return` / `sure-shot profit`
- `fixed profit` / `fixed return` (prohibited for market-linked equities; permitted only when referencing regulatory Fixed Income debt instruments)
- `100% profit` / `100% return`
- `100% safe` / `100% secure` / `absolutely safe`
- `no loss` / `zero loss` / `loss-proof`
- `risk-free investment` / `risk-free trading` / `risk-free profit`
- `guaranteed strategy` / `guaranteed signal` / `guaranteed setup`
- `guaranteed target` / `guaranteed entry` / `guaranteed exit`
- `always profitable` / `never loses` / `never fails` / `works every time`
- `certain profit` / `definite profit`

**Restricted Transliterated & Devanagari Equivalents:**
- `guaranteed return` / गारंटीकृत रिटर्न / पक्का मुनाफा / निश्चित रिटर्न / सुनिश्चित रिटर्न
- `100% safe` / 100% सुरक्षित / शत-प्रतिशत सुरक्षित
- `zero loss` / शून्य नुकसान / कोई नुकसान नहीं (when claiming an equity strategy carries no downside)
- `risk-free investment` / जोखिम-मुक्त निवेश / बिना किसी जोखिम के मुनाफा

**Crucial Contextual Exception (Level 0 — Acceptable):**
Educational negations that actively disprove or warn against guarantees are **Level 0 Acceptable**.
- *"No strategy can guarantee profits."* (Level 0)
- *"Equities carry market risks; there are no guaranteed returns."* (Level 0)
- *"Past performance does not guarantee future results."* (Level 0)

---

### Category 2: Market Prediction & Certainty Claims (Level 2 to Level 3)
Claims asserting that market price movements can be forecasted with infallible certainty.

**Restricted Phrases:**
- `will definitely rise` / `will definitely fall` (Level 3)
- `must rise` / `must fall` (Level 3)
- `cannot fall` / `cannot lose` / `cannot go wrong` (Level 3)
- `guaranteed prediction` / `exact prediction` (Level 3)
- `100% accurate signal` / `foolproof setup` (Level 3)

**Devanagari Equivalents:**
- निश्चित रूप से बढ़ेगा / अवश्य बढ़ेगा / कभी असफल नहीं होता / अचूक संकेतक

**Contextual Exception on "Sure" and "Certain" (Level 0 — Acceptable):**
Do NOT globally ban "sure" or "certain". Non-promotional, pedagogical, or conditional uses are fully approved:
- *"Are you sure you want to exit?"* → Level 0
- *"Under certain market conditions, liquidity may decline."* → Level 0
- *"A certain outcome in this hypothetical probability exercise..."* → Level 0
- *RESTRICTED:* *"Sure profit"* (Level 3) or *"Certain to rise"* (Level 3).

---

### Category 3: Gambling & Colloquial Speculation Terminology (Level 3 — Zero Tolerance)
Stock market investing is a formal mechanism of capital formation and business ownership. Colloquial gambling terminology degrades financial literacy.

**The Satta Family (Zero-Tolerance Hard Block):**
- English / Transliterated: `satta`, `satte`, `sattebaji`, `sattabazi`, `sattabaji`, `satta market`, `satta bazar`, `satta trading`
- Devanagari: सट्टा, सट्टे, सट्टेबाजी, सट्टेबाज़, सट्टा बाज़ार

**STRICT ENFORCEMENT RULE:**
1. Zero occurrences of the Satta family are permitted anywhere in production course content, including introductory descriptions, myth cards, or options.
2. **Replacement Rule:** An author or tool must **NEVER replace a prohibited satta term with another satta-derived term**.
   - *Prohibited Replacement:* `"अत्यधिक सट्टा"` (Violates policy by reintroducing satta).
   - *Approved Replacement:* `"अत्यधिक जोखिम वाला (High-Risk Speculative)"` or `"अनियंत्रित स्पेक्युलेशन (Unregulated Speculation)"`.

**Other Gambling Tokens:**
- `gambling` / `gamble` / जुआ / जुआबाजी
- `betting` / `bet` / दांव लगाना (when framed as gambling rather than capital allocation)
- `jackpot` / `jackpot share` / जैकपॉट
- `casino` / `casino market` / कैसीनो

---

### Category 4: Contextual Treatment of "Lottery" (`लॉटरी`)
"Lottery" must be evaluated contextually rather than under a blanket ban.

1. **IPO Allocation Context (Level 2 — Rewrite Required):**
   - Under SEBI ICDR Regulations, the retail oversubscription allotment process is formally a **"Computerized Draw of Lots"**.
   - Using colloquial terms like *"लॉटरी अलॉटमेंट"* or *"लॉटरी ड्रा"* in teaching explanations should be rewritten to:
     - **`कंप्यूटरीकृत ड्रॉ (Computerized Draw of Lots)`**
     - **`अलॉटमेंट प्रायिकता (Allotment Probability)`** (instead of `Lottery Odds`)
2. **Negative Quiz Distractor Context (Level 0 — Acceptable):**
   - When "lottery" is used in an intentionally incorrect multiple-choice distractor to test whether a student incorrectly believes exchange order matching or capital markets operate like a lottery:
   - Example: *Option D: "यादृच्छिक (Random) लॉटरी द्वारा"* (Lesson 14 Quiz).
   - **Verdict: Level 0 — Fully Acceptable**. Do not fail the build for negative quiz distractors that test misconceptions.

### Category 5: Legitimate Financial Terminology (Level 0 — Protected)
The scanner and review gates must explicitly recognize and protect standard financial, economic, and quantitative terms:

1. **`Risk-Free Rate` / `Risk-Free Rate of Return` (जोखिम-मुक्त दर):**
   - Standard international benchmark referring strictly to sovereign short-term debt (e.g., 91-day Government of India Treasury Bills) used in the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) and Sharpe Ratio calculations.
   - **Verdict: Level 0 — Legitimate Terminology.**
2. **`Cost of Capital` (पूंजी की लागत):**
   - Core corporate finance concept. **Verdict: Level 0.**
3. **`Speculation` / `Speculative Trading` (स्पेक्युलेशन):**
   - Legitimate academic economic term distinguishing high-turnover, short-term price risk from multi-year fundamental investing. **Verdict: Level 0.**
4. **`Fixed Income` (फिक्स्ड इनकम):**
   - Regulatory asset class category covering government bonds, debentures, and commercial paper. **Verdict: Level 0.**
5. **`Break-Even (No Profit, No Loss)` (ना लाभ, ना हानि):**
   - Fundamental arithmetic threshold where Net Revenue equals Total Costs. **Verdict: Level 0.**

---

### Category 6: Technical Indicators & Lagging Tools (Level 2 to Level 3)
Applies to Lessons 17 through 20 (Charts, Candlesticks, Trends, and EMA/RSI/VWAP).

**Mandated Framing:**
- Indicators must be described as **lagging, historical, descriptive, or analytical tools**.
- Prohibited: Describing an indicator as a *"predictive machine"*, *"guaranteed entry/exit"*, or *"win-rate guarantee"*.

**Classification:**
- *"200 EMA helps identify historical long-term trend bias."* → Level 0
- *"RSI indicates momentum and historical overbought zones."* → Level 0
- *"RSI guarantees a reversal at level 30."* → Level 3 (Hard Block)

---

### Category 7: Direct Advisory & Personalized Recommendations (Level 3 — Hard Block)
Course content is educational and must never offer actionable investment calls.

**Prohibited Phrases:**
- `buy this stock` / `sell this stock` / `buy now` / `sell now`
- `you should buy` / `you must buy` / `you must sell`
- `target guaranteed` / `profit target guaranteed`
- `stock recommendation` / `hot tip`
