3. Frequently Asked Questions (Quantitative Intelligence)

What does it mean when FIIs are 79.87% net short in Index Futures?

When Foreign Institutional Investors hold 79.87% short contracts in index futures, it reflects substantial hedging of cash equity holdings. In this state, sharp rallies frequently occur when FIIs cover short contracts, while retail longs face elevated vulnerability to pullbacks.

How does delivery percentage differentiate smart money from retail churn?

Intraday retail and algorithmic scalping volume cancels out by 3:30 PM. Delivery percentage measures the actual quantity of shares transferred to custodial Demat accounts. Delivery volume above 60% with hundreds of crores in committed turnover signals long-term institutional sponsorship.

What is the Retail-FII Divergence Index?

It is the net mathematical gap between retail long contracts and institutional long contracts. The current divergence of +50.04% highlights that retail positioning is heavily bullish while smart money remains hedged.
